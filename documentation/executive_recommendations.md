# Executive Recommendations

1. Prioritize categories with high addressable spend, medium-or-higher confidence, and manageable risk.
2. Use Balanced scenarios as the default recommendation, then document approved deviations.
3. Review categories with top-supplier share above the concentration threshold before consolidating suppliers.
4. Convert off-contract spend into category sourcing initiatives and contracting actions.
5. Establish a monthly value-realization review covering stage progression and leakage reasons.
6. Separate negotiated savings from implemented and realized savings in executive reporting.
7. Introduce supplier early-warning indicators for categories with high spend concentration.
8. Require Finance validation before opportunities move from Approved to Negotiated and Realized.
