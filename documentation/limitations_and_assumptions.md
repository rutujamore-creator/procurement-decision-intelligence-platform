# Limitations and Assumptions

- All records and outcomes are synthetic.
- FX rates are simplified fixed conversion factors.
- Scenario outputs are transparent rule-based assumptions, not optimization-model results.
- External commodity, inflation, sanctions, financial, ESG, and geopolitical risk data is excluded.
- Price-volume-mix analysis is demonstrated as a methodology and requires item-level comparability in production.
- Real savings require Finance-approved baselines, implementation evidence, and realized P&L impact.
- A production solution requires access controls, lineage, refresh monitoring, UAT, change control, and benefit governance.
