# Business Requirements

## Stakeholders
CPO, Procurement Transformation Lead, Category Managers, Finance Business Partner, Supplier Risk Lead, BI Product Owner.

## Requirements
- Create a 2024-2025 spend cube across supplier, category, geography, plant, and business unit.
- Detect off-contract and maverick spend.
- Quantify supplier fragmentation and concentration.
- Rank sourcing opportunities using savings, confidence, and risk.
- Compare Maximum Savings, Balanced, and Maximum Resilience award scenarios.
- Track savings from Identified through Realized.
- Explain leakage and maintain ownership, stage, and target dates.
- Reconcile transaction counts and total spend to source data.

## Acceptance Criteria
- All monetary outputs reconcile to source spend.
- Scenario assumptions are explicit and configurable.
- Recommended scenarios include trade-offs, not only maximum savings.
- Executive recommendations link to opportunity IDs and categories.
