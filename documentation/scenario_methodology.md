# Scenario Methodology

## Maximum Savings
Prioritizes benefit and supplier consolidation. Accepts higher concentration and implementation risk.

## Balanced
Optimizes savings while applying moderate supplier-count, quality, delivery, and concentration constraints. This is the recommended scenario in the synthetic model.

## Maximum Resilience
Prioritizes supplier diversification, delivery continuity, and quality, with lower expected savings.

## Assumptions
Scenario savings rates, concentration shares, supplier counts, and quality or delivery impacts are synthetic assumptions. They must be replaced by category-specific evidence in a real implementation.
