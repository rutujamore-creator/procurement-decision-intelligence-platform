# Five-Minute Interview Walkthrough

1. **Problem:** Procurement leaders need decisions, not only spend visibility.
2. **Model:** Transaction spend, supplier performance, contract, scenario, and savings-pipeline facts.
3. **Opportunity engine:** Addressable spend, estimated benefit, confidence, and risk.
4. **Trade-offs:** Compare Maximum Savings, Balanced, and Maximum Resilience scenarios.
5. **Value realization:** Track savings from Identified to Realized and explain leakage.
6. **Recommendation:** Prioritize balanced, risk-adjusted opportunities and govern stage progression.
7. **Limitations:** Synthetic assumptions are transparent and would be replaced with category evidence.
